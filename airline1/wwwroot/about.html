About Badr Airlines

Badr Airlines is a leading airline committed to connecting people and places across Africa, the Middle East, and beyond. Founded in 2004, we have grown to become a trusted name in aviation, known for our reliability, safety, and customer service.

Our Mission

To provide safe, comfortable, and affordable air travel while fostering connections between cultures and communities.

Our Values

  • Safety: Our top priority in every flight.
  • Customer Focus: We strive to exceed passenger expectations.
  • Innovation: Embracing new technologies for a better travel experience.
  • Integrity: Operating with honesty and transparency.

Our Achievements

  • Over 2 million passengers flown annually.
  • Expanding network to over 20 destinations.
  • Recipient of the "Best Regional Airline" award in 2022.

Meet Our Team

Our dedicated team of professionals is passionate about delivering the best possible service to our passengers, from the ground staff to our experienced pilots and cabin crew.
